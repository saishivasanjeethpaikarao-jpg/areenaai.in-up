DescriptionInspired by a mix of classical Roman capitals and the intriguing shapes of Summerian cuneiform script, Curia is the result of an exploration of modularity and serif fonts. The lowercase characters follow a mostly strict mono-spaced design while the drawing of the uppercase characters follows more traditional letterforms, and thus a more proportional style; each case can be seen as its own system.

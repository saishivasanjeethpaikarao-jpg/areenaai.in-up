Recognizing this, we commit ourselves to being grateful and respectful towards one another. We are a group of curious, optimistic, and enthusiastic individuals who deeply care about all of our initiatives. Feeling proud of what we do is the energy that fuels us, that keeps us motivated and excited. ### Push Together as a Team We are characterized by our freshness and good sense of humor. We enjoy sharing, having fun, and celebrating moments together. However, we also take the time to reflect and identify what we have learned, as well as acknowledge our mistakes when things do not go well. We strive to act as one, always together. ### Improve by 1% Every Day We believe that recognizing our own limitations helps us identify opportunities and encourages teamwork. Each of us is a specialist and shines in our unique way. We welcome challenges, persist through obstacles, and learn from our mistakes. This motivates us to ask for help when needed and be accountable to each other. If you're passionate enough to help shape one of the best creative studios in the world, and you never settle when faced with new challenges, you might just belong here. We're looking for curious, optimistic, and driven people who love pushing boundaries, thinking big, and executing with heart.

Frontend Developer

Careers

Development

Full Time

Remote

As a Frontend Developer @ basement, your mission is to build polished, fast, and accessible web experiences for both humans and agents. Attention to detail really matters. Every interface we ship should reflect high standards in performance, usability, and visual quality.

At Basement, we believe creativity is the main driving force for real impact. We’re building our dream place to work: a chill, safe, high-standard environment where talented, curious, and passionate people come together to create without limits and to build great businesses along the way.

We work at the intersection of creativity, content, and technology, crafting emotionally-driven digital experiences for global audiences, top brands, and fast-growing startups. We don’t settle. We’re intentional, detail-oriented, and always pushing the bar higher with trust, autonomy, and healthy demanding standards as our foundation.

About the role

You’ll work with top-tier clients like Vercel, Coinbase, Mr. Beast, and Cursor, which means the bar is high —both visually and technically.

We’re looking for someone who takes pride in their craft and is ready to rise to the moments that demand more, because the work we do isn’t average.

You’ll collaborate closely with designers and developers, learn from each other, and help raise the bar for everyone around you.

The position is fully remote. Most of our team works in GMT-3 (ART), and our clients are mostly based in PT, so some daily overlap between the two time zones is expected.

What you’ll do

  • Build high-quality, performant web experiences from design to production.
  • Collaborate closely with designers to translate visual concepts into polished, pixel-perfect interfaces.
  • Implement complex layouts, responsive systems, and high-quality UI interactions.
  • Contribute to improving our frontend architecture, tooling, and best practices.

What we’re looking for

  • High agency mindset — you move things forward, solve problems, and take ownership of your work.
  • Strong attention to detail.
  • Comfortable working in a collaborative environment with designers and engineers.
  • Fluent English for day-to-day communication with clients.

Tech Stack

  • Strong experience with React, Next.js, and TypeScript, with a strong understanding of application architecture, state management strategies, and performance optimization.
  • Complete understanding of Next.js ecosystem, including React Server Components, App Router, and rendering strategies like SSR, ISR, SSG, and PPR.
  • Experience working with data fetching, caching strategies, route handlers, revalidation, and streaming.
  • Strong command of Tailwind CSS and modern CSS fundamentals, with the ability to implement clean, pixel-perfect UIs, complex layouts, and smooth UI interactions.
  • Experience integrating headless CMS platforms such as Sanity or Payload, with a focus on modular and scalable architectures.
  • Experience building animations using GSAP and/or Motion.

What we offer

  • Remote work environment
  • US holidays
  • 2 Weeks Vacations
  • 1 Week off between Christmas & New Year
  • Day off per quarter
  • Birthday off
  • English group lessons
  • Annual Company Retreat
  • Office in Mar del Plata & Coworking Sessions in Buenos Aires

# Speakeasy: On-Site, On-Point, Built to Scale **Client:** [Speakeasy](https://www.speakeasy.com/) **Year:** 2025 **Categories:** Visual Branding, Websites & Features **Website:** https://www.speakeasy.com/ --- Speakeasy builds tools devs love. The SDKs, the docs, the workflows: clean, fast, and obsessive about clarity. We wanted the brand to feel the same way. Their founding designer flew in from the UK and joined us on-site for two weeks of deep work in Argentina. It was all about fast alignment and faster decisions. We crafted a fresh, minimalistic visual identity, one built to scale with the product, not just sit on top of it. Every element was designed with engineers in mind: modular, precise, and designed to enhance the overall product experience.

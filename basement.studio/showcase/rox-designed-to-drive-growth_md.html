# Rox: Designed to Drive Growth **Client:** [Rox](https://www.rox.com/) **Year:** 2025 **Categories:** Websites & Features **Website:** https://www.rox.com/ --- Rox powers revenue teams at some of the world’s biggest companies. But while the product kept evolving, their website still looked like an early-stage startup. They needed something that matched their scale and showed the sophistication and maturity behind their product. The new site gave their marketing team the foundation they needed to tell customer stories, link real results, and turn every page into a conversation starter. Since launch, the impact has been clear: from 9K active users the month before launch to 39K the month after. Beyond the numbers, the new site made Rox feel more credible, more robust, elevated and ready for enterprise scale.

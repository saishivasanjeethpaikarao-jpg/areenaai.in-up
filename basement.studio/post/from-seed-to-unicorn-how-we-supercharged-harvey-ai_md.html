# From Seed to Unicorn: How we Supercharged Harvey AI **Author:** Natalia Martinez Lopez **Published:** 5/4/2024 **Categories:** Case Study --- We partnered with Harvey AI to craft a brand and website that propelled them from seed stage to a $1.5 billion valuation (and counting), supporting their mission to transform the legal industry with AI. When we first crossed paths with [Harvey](https://harvey.ai/)[ AI](https://harvey.ai/) in late 2022, they were a seed-stage startup with a bold vision: to reshape the legal industry using the most powerful AI platform ever built for law. Harvey was on the brink of something big, but they needed a brand identity and a digital presence that could match their groundbreaking ambitions. ## The Challenge [Gabriel Pereyra](https://gabepereyra.com/) and [Winston Weinberg](https://www.forbes.com/profile/winston-weinberg/), the masterminds behind Harvey, were moving at lightning speed. Gabriel, with his AI chops honed at Google Brain and Meta, and Winston, a seasoned attorney, were already making their mark. However, they needed a cohesive brand identity and a compelling web presence to support their traction and fundraising efforts. That’s where we came in. ## **The basement** App**roach** We enjoy tackling the challenges that push others to their limits. We dove headfirst into Harvey's world, crafting a lean yet scalable brand system in record time. It wasn't just about making something that looked good—it was about creating a brand that could grow with them, adapting to the demands of a rapidly evolving market. We then turned our focus to their digital front door: the website. In true basement style, we delivered a small website that didn’t just look sharp—it became a powerful tool in their fundraising arsenal, setting the stage for their next big leap. ![Image](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/a96b319a28410581b3a4afc3e596b76616a5c685-3840x2167.png?w=2400&h=2400&fit=max&auto=format) As Harvey continued to scale, so did our support. We worked closely with their product team, ensuring that the brand's aesthetic was seamlessly integrated into their core product, resulting in a consistent, cohesive experience across the board. ## Sustaining Growth Our partnership didn’t stop there. From micro-animations to product demo videos, we supported Harvey’s day-to-day marketing design needs, helping them maintain their momentum and stay ahead of the competition. Today, Harvey is more than just a startup—they’re a trusted name in legal tech, leading the charge with a secure generative AI platform that’s become indispensable to law firms, in-house teams, and financial professionals. ## The Outcome In just two years of working with basement, Harvey's growth has been exceptionally rapid. From a $5 million seed round in November 2022 to a $21 million Series A in April 2023, and by June 2024, they closed a $100 million at a valuation of $1.5 billion. Their revenue surged over 10x, and they’ve secured backing from some of the biggest names in venture capital, including [Elad](https://eladgil.com/)[ Gil](https://eladgil.com/), [Kleiner Perkins,](https://www.kleinerperkins.com/) [OpenAI](https://openai.com/), and [Sequoia](https://www.sequoiacap.com/). To cap it all off, we recently launched their brand new website, featuring a complete design system refresh to resonate with their growing audience. It’s a fitting milestone in a partnership that’s been all about growth, innovation, and pushing boundaries. > basement hit our unrealistic deadlines, worked with little to no guidance, and designed the brand and website we hoped for even when we couldn't fully articulate it.

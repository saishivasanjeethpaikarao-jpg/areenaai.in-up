# Designing Dynaboard: Collaboration Simplified **Author:** Natalia Martinez Lopez **Published:** 7/7/2021 **Categories:** Case Study --- Dynaboard is reshaping **collaborative web development**, and we built a site to match—fast, dynamic, and effortlessly intuitive. ## Revolutionizing the world of collaborative web development [Dynaboard](https://dynaboard.com/) is the renegade altering the norm of what it means to collaborate on software, with all the bells and whistles, it’s a utility belt Batman wishes he had. Their goal, to build the next generation of the web "together" in one place, having one source of truth, and reclaiming time spent on debating frameworks and deployments. Disrupting collaborative software development on a planetary scale. ![Image](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/250596cdd4642233eadc6e9067d1e44072ad6c4a-2452x1966.webp?w=2400&h=2400&fit=max&auto=format) ## Expectations were high and possibilities were endless We were charged with turning an idea into reality and building the face of a new and collaborative web. We were given absolute freedom to explore different designs which opened us up to a multiverse of possibilities. We had to approach this with an open mind, balancing between fun and passing along the values of Dynaboard to its audience. ![Image](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/52aaba1c58f011fb12c93e17cc8a48550c1353ee-3840x2160.webp?w=2400&h=2400&fit=max&auto=format) ## Dynaboard cares, and so do we We had to find the sweet spot. Minimal, simple, colorful, and powerful. We transferred the same energy and practicality of the platform into the website design and made it just as exciting. We inserted with the Dynaboard team to amass valuable intel from existing design assets to facilitate brand and creative direction for the website.001002**Turning web development into a fun experience.** ![Image](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/07ab662b55290f062806441e7474b13f015efc5e-2452x1745.webp?w=2400&h=2400&fit=max&auto=format) We created a front-end experience that was fun at its core and well structured. The website was built to adapt to changes in the CMS with high-speed delivery using GraphQL bundled with the power from Next.js.

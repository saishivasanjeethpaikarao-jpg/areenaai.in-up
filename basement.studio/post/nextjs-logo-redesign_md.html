# Next.js Logo Redesign **Author:** Mateo Zaragoza **Published:** 9/8/2022 **Categories:** Design --- As Next.js evolved, so did its need for a sharper, more adaptable logo. Partnering with Vercel, we refined the mark for better usability, scalability, and recognition—built for the future. ## Forging a Competitive Edge In a rapidly evolving digital landscape, [Next.js](https://Next.js) introduced a new paradigm and quickly gained the trust of industry leaders on the web. To maintain its competitive edge, Vercel identified the need for a brand revitalization. It was time to encapsulate the reliability and efficiency that [Next.js](https://Next.js) signifies through a new logo, driven by the strength of scalability and an emphasis on simplicity - one that excels not just in form but also in function. ![Next.js logo before and after](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/47a53b569c7eb99c442f3066e59c5c864a9fb34f-3840x2161.jpg?w=2400&h=2400&fit=max&auto=format) _Next.js logo before and after_ ## A Reshape For The Future We needed a new shape that could adapt to the complexities of the web and its ever-shifting dynamics of size, space, and resolution. The previous [Next.js](https://Next.js) logo faced two significant issues: its usability was problematic in many scenarios, and it became hard to read in small sizes, even to the point of breaking down visually. ![Next.js symbol figure asset](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/fad5e42c83761d4960e99907c4d2d72efd2ec672-870x742.jpg) _Next.js symbol figure asset_ ![Simple grid figure asset](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/c21705392d73e00f13d46ce8977eefed50bb071f-2452x1964.jpg?w=2400&h=2400&fit=max&auto=format) _Simple grid figure asset_ ## A Bold Move For Next.js With close collaboration with the Vercel design team, we've ended up with a reliable solution that captures the speed conveyed by the previous logo and emphasizes it through functionality. We looked for high legibility and contrasting presence for the new approach, making the shape quickly stand out everywhere. ![Grid and Angles](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/211002fb68a14791f8f9d1dbb57c34702ef23f02-3840x2161.jpg?w=2400&h=2400&fit=max&auto=format) _Grid and Angles_ While embracing new paradigms, the design team recognized the importance of honoring the brand's established identity. This balance ensured that the legacy of [Next.js](https://Next.js) remained intact while aligning with the company's forward-looking vision. The blend between heritage and innovation resonated with the business's drive to cater to both existing and emerging markets. ![Big asset](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/a6ae4405ee230e0b6e5f52a76d66ecf67daeafc9-3840x2161.jpg?w=2400&h=2400&fit=max&auto=format) _Big asset_ ## From Solid Foundations At basement, we love building on the web because it often resembles a retro-futuristic journey. And that’s why we admire how [Next.js](https://Next.js) embraces the future by drawing on the foundational principles that shaped the early days of the internet. It's not a surprise that our inspiration spaned from the ventures of Steve Jobs and Paul Rand in the 80s all the way to modern innovators like SpaceX, Tesla and beyond. ![Early mockup figure asset](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/56bf59644b901060e57277cc567e305a5b8c0154-870x742.jpg) _Early mockup figure asset_ ![The moodboard figure asset](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/616ccc5c03e007c9f22114171c347f74a4320710-2452x1964.jpg?w=2400&h=2400&fit=max&auto=format) _The moodboard figure asset_ > We spotted a retro-futuristic direction right from the start. The hard work was refining it. > > — Jose Rago, Co-Founder From the start, our path was clear. Using simple grids and shapes, we moved from tech-style to sci-fi, aiming for a new, unique look while staying within the rules of straight lines and sharp angles. ![Next.js Logo Early Exploration](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/198bb0cf10443be7c386f8d09fa3eb993a49c138-1920x1081.jpg) _Next.js Logo Early Exploration_ ## An evolving process Once we had a clear concept of the desired outcome, we continued experimenting with the logo hand-to-hand with the Vercel team. We focused on adjusting its boldness and kerning, testing different notches and cuts for each letter. ![Next.js Logo Process](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/0abac18361d59afb55aeac92e63082f271b879f2-3840x2161.jpg?w=2400&h=2400&fit=max&auto=format) _Next.js Logo Process_ We even experimented with some versions that echoed the previous logo. This process revealed the opportunity to resemble the ideas of the original shapes through bolder explorations of the new design. We were keen to capture the intriguing dynamic between the 'N' and 'X.’ ![Big asset](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/1f9e77a9d7245922bd210c048db2c60a69508a08-3840x2161.jpg?w=2400&h=2400&fit=max&auto=format) _Big asset_ ## The Missing Part As [Next.js](https://Next.js) is a prime example of an MVP in the open-source world, polishing the finer details wasn't a top priority. When your focus is making the web faster, the first step is shipping, right? Sure, the journey began with just the original wordmark. But when success came knocking, the need for a complete brand overhaul was clear. We're thrilled that the time has finally come to dive into the basement to add the final touch - the missing symbol. ![Big asset](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/55eb0b1c35fa54f5fc9e85da4e67bef788f425da-3840x2161.jpg?w=2400&h=2400&fit=max&auto=format) _Big asset_ ## The next standard Vercel wanted to ship this logo for one of the major product releases in 2022. The deadline was pressing, and the expected exposure was immense. But isn't that the ideal recipe for success? Indeed, when the logo was finally revealed at the conference's opening, the immediate feedback confirmed that this bold move was not just necessary but transformative. ![Image](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/eea5e564401d963e6e5758a2aff9f9aaf6080273-1277x719.jpg) ![Image](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/64c254fe042a0102994d77b102198b04ca73d683-1277x719.jpg) ![Image](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/9syto90m/production/14212a622505813dfde4c70cfba35601a84a954a-1277x719.jpg) With just a few months in the field, it’s rewarding to see how the new logo allows a wide variety of usages and creativity. By addressing usability issues, enhancing brand recognition, and resonating with a broader audience, the redesign aligns Next.js's visual identity with Vercel’s consolidation in the industry.
